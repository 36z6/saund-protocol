# WHY SAUND EXISTS
by 36z6

Most AI music is slop. Not because the models are bad — because we talk
to them badly. We throw adjectives at an 800-character field and hope.
The model guesses. The guess is average. Average is slop.

I spent a year doing the opposite: treating a prompt not as a wish but
as a specification. Sections as code. Timbres as typed slots. Effects
as registered symbols with declared capabilities. Budgets as law.
Silence as a control, not an accident. When the input is deterministic,
the output stops being a lottery.

SAUND is that practice, written down. It is a compiler between human
intent and a generative audio backend: a declarative document goes in,
validated and synchronized Style/Lyrics fields come out, with a legend
that lets any reader decode every single letter.

I release it as a gift. No company, no fee, no roadmap owned by anyone
but the people who use it. If it helps one person make a track that
sounds like a decision instead of a guess — that is the whole return
I ask for.

Three promises baked into the spec:
1. Nothing is silent. Every override, fallback and compression is logged.
2. Nothing is invented. If a backend cannot do it, the spec says so aloud.
3. Nothing is lost. Your idea enters as truth and leaves as projection,
   traceable end to end.

Make music better than average. The models were ready. We were not.

— 36z6
